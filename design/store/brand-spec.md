# Tracend brand spec

Tokens: `--bg #F3F6F8`, `--surface #FFFFFF`, `--fg #10151D`, `--muted #556170`, `--border #D5DDE5`, `--accent #4A57E8`.

- Display/UI titles: `Spline Sans`, 500/600/700.
- Data and numeric values: `IBM Plex Mono`, 400/500/600.
- Body copy: system sans stack.
- Campaign pages use a light polar canvas with a high-key indigo arena spotlight and sparse tapered motion trails.
- App screens use a dark “Precision Pro” theme with raised slate surfaces, hairline borders, and indigo/teal semantic states.
- The phone is the visual anchor; type is oversized, uppercase, and tightly tracked above it.
